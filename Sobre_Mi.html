About me

Born on October 8, 1999 in Nicaragua, Gustav Hooker has grown up in an environment rich in culture and traditions. From an early age, he showed a keen interest in cooking, which led him to follow his passion and study gastronomy. His dedication and love for food led him to specialize in Italian cuisine, a culinary art that combines flavors, techniques, and a rich history. Throughout his training, Gustav has learned to master the secrets of fresh pasta, traditional sauces and irresistible desserts, becoming a true ambassador of Italian cuisine in his community. His creativity and skill in the kitchen have not only helped him develop his career, but have also allowed him to share his love of gastronomy with friends and family.

Deportes Favoritos

Basketball

I played basketball for approximately 5 to 6 years, a stage that profoundly marked my life and my way of understanding the sport. Throughout that time, I not only developed technical and physical skills, but also a deep understanding of the game in strategic, tactical, and emotional terms. Thanks to that experience, I consider myself a knowledgeable person about basketball, both on and off the court.

American football

Football has always been a sport that I deeply enjoy, even if I have never practiced it formally. It appeals to me because of its intensity, its tactical complexity and the passion it arouses in both players and fans. Despite not having played it, I have spent time learning about its rules, formations, strategies and the functioning of each position on the field.

volleyball

I played volleyball for about 2 years, an experience that allowed me to develop not only physical and technical skills, but also a great passion for this sport. Throughout that time, I learned how to work as a team, communicate effectively on the court, and maintain a mindset focused on both competition and continuous improvement.

Baseball

Entrené béisbol durante un período significativo, desempeñándome principalmente como pitcher, una posición que requiere no solo habilidad física, sino también concentración, estrategia y liderazgo en el campo. Esta experiencia me permitió conocer el deporte desde una perspectiva muy técnica y táctica, desarrollando un entendimiento profundo del juego y de sus dinámicas. Como lanzador, aprendí a controlar el ritmo del partido, a tomar decisiones rápidas bajo presión y a estudiar a los bateadores para anticipar sus movimientos. También trabajé en la mecánica del lanzamiento, el control, la velocidad y la precisión, aspectos fundamentales para tener un desempeño competitivo en esa posición.
